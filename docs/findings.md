# Project Findings

## Saudi Data Internship and Job Market Analysis

This document summarizes the main findings identified from analyzing Saudi data-related internship and job postings.

---

## 1. Required Tools Analysis

The analysis showed that SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau are among the most frequently requested tools in Saudi data-related opportunities.

These results indicate that students preparing for data careers should focus on:

- SQL for database querying and data management.
- Python for data analysis and automation.
- Power BI and Excel for reporting and visualization.
- Tableau for data visualization and reporting tasks.

---

## 2. Required Skills Analysis

The analysis identified several important skills required in Saudi data-related roles.

The most common skills include:

- Data Analysis.
- Reporting.
- Problem Solving.
- Data Governance.
- Data Validation.
- Data Integrity.
- Dashboard Development.
- Data Visualization.
- Business Intelligence.
- Machine Learning.

These findings show that data careers require a combination of technical abilities, analytical thinking, and business understanding.

---

## 3. City Distribution Analysis

The dataset analysis showed that Riyadh contains the highest number of data-related opportunities compared with other cities.

This indicates that Riyadh represents a major hub for data internships and early-career roles in Saudi Arabia.

Other cities also contain data-related opportunities, but with lower frequency in the collected dataset.

---

## 4. Role Category Analysis

The dataset contains multiple data career pathways, including:

- Data Analysis.
- Business Intelligence.
- Data Science.
- AI-related roles.
- Data Governance.
- Analytics and reporting roles.
- Internship opportunities.

This highlights that students can enter the data field through different career paths depending on their skills, interests, and career goals.

---

## 5. Experience Level Analysis

The analysis showed different experience requirements across postings, including:

- Internship opportunities.
- Entry-level roles.
- Mid-level positions.
- Senior roles.
- Not Specified opportunities.

Understanding experience requirements helps students identify suitable opportunities and prepare according to their career stage.

For students preparing for internships and early-career roles, focusing on practical projects, technical foundations, and portfolio development can improve readiness.

---

## 6. Overall Findings

Based on the analysis, a strong foundation for Saudi data-related careers should include:

- SQL.
- Python.
- Data analysis.
- Data visualization.
- Power BI.
- Database concepts.
- Practical projects.
- Portfolio development.

The findings from this analysis were used to develop a structured readiness roadmap for Computer Science students interested in data careers.

---

## Conclusion

The project findings demonstrate that Saudi data-related opportunities require a combination of technical skills, analytical capabilities, and practical experience.

By understanding market requirements and prioritizing the most demanded skills and tools, students can create a clearer learning path toward data internships and early-career opportunities.
