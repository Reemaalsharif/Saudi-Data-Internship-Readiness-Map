# Saudi Data Internship Readiness Dashboard

## Overview

This dashboard visualizes insights from 45 Saudi data-related job postings to understand market requirements and identify the most requested skills, tools, roles, cities, and experience levels for data-related careers.

The dashboard transforms job market data into clear visual insights that help Computer Science students understand the skills and technologies required for data internships and early-career opportunities.

---

## Dataset

The dashboard is built using the cleaned Saudi Data Jobs dataset containing **45 job postings** collected from different data-related career categories.

The dataset includes information about:

- Job roles.
- Required skills.
- Required tools.
- Locations.
- Experience requirements.

Additional analysis-ready columns were created to support dashboard development, including:

- `city_cleaned`
- `role_category`
- `experience_category`

---

## Dashboard Objectives

The main objectives of this dashboard are:

- Identify the most requested technical skills in Saudi data-related roles.
- Analyze the most common tools and technologies required by employers.
- Understand job distribution across different Saudi cities.
- Explore different data career role categories.
- Analyze experience levels required for available opportunities.

---

# Dashboard Visualizations

## 1. Skills Analysis

This visualization shows the most frequently requested skills in Saudi data-related job postings.

It helps identify the skills that are most important for students preparing for data careers.

---

## 2. Tools Analysis

This visualization presents the most commonly required tools and technologies.

It highlights important platforms and programming tools that frequently appear in data job requirements.

---

## 3. City Distribution

This visualization analyzes the distribution of data-related opportunities across Saudi cities.

It helps identify locations with higher demand for data-related roles.

---

## 4. Role Category Analysis

This visualization shows the distribution of different data-related job categories.

It helps understand the main career paths available, including Data Analysis, Business Intelligence, Data Science, and other analytics-related roles.

---

## 5. Experience Level Analysis

This visualization analyzes the experience levels required by employers.

It provides insights into entry-level and more advanced career expectations.

---

# Key Insights

The dashboard highlights several important findings:

- Data analysis skills are among the most requested skills in Saudi data-related roles.
- Python, SQL, and visualization tools are important requirements for data careers.
- Job opportunities are concentrated in major Saudi cities.
- Different career paths require different combinations of skills and tools.
- The findings help identify the gap between student preparation and market expectations.

---

# Conclusion

This dashboard provides a visual overview of the Saudi data job market by analyzing required skills, tools, roles, locations, and experience levels.

The insights support the development of a practical career readiness roadmap for Computer Science students preparing for data internships and early-career opportunities.
