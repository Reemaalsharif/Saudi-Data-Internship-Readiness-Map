# Saudi Data Internship Readiness Dashboard

## Overview
This dashboard analyzes Saudi data internship and job postings to understand market requirements and identify the most important skills, tools, roles, cities, and experience levels required for data-related careers.

## Dataset
The dashboard is built using a cleaned dataset of Saudi data internship and job postings. The dataset contains information about job roles, required skills, tools, locations, and experience requirements.

## Dashboard Objectives
The main objectives of this dashboard are:
- Identify the most requested technical skills.
- Analyze the most common tools required in data-related jobs.
- Understand job distribution across different cities.
- Explore different job role categories.
- Analyze experience levels required by employers.

## Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Skills Analysis
This chart shows the most frequently requested skills in Saudi data internship and job postings. It helps identify the skills that are most important for students preparing for data careers.

### 2. Tools Analysis
This chart presents the most commonly required tools and technologies. It highlights the software and platforms that appear frequently in job requirements.

### 3. City Distribution
This visualization analyzes the distribution of data opportunities across Saudi cities and shows where most opportunities are concentrated.

### 4. Role Category Analysis
This chart shows different job role categories and their frequency in the dataset, helping understand the main career paths available in data-related fields.

### 5. Experience Level Analysis
This visualization analyzes the required experience levels for available opportunities and shows the expectations of employers.

## Key Insights
- Data analysis skills are among the most requested skills in the market.
- Python, SQL, and visualization tools are important requirements for data roles.
- Job opportunities are concentrated in major Saudi cities.
- Different career paths require different combinations of skills and tools.

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides insights into the Saudi data job market and helps students build a roadmap by focusing on the most demanded skills, tools, and requirements.
